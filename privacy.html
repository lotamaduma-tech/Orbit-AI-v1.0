The information Orbit handles depends on the features you use.

  • Account creation and authentication-related information.

Orbit may allow you to provide profile information, such as a display name.

Profile information may be stored in connection with your Orbit account and used to provide account and personalization features.

You can modify available profile information through the account controls provided by Orbit.

4. Conversations and Messages

When conversation and history functionality is available, Orbit may process and store information contained in your conversations.

This may include:

  • Messages you send to Orbit.
  • Responses generated by Orbit.
  • Conversation titles.
  • Conversation identifiers.
  • User or account identifiers.
  • Creation and update timestamps.

Depending on the feature and implementation, conversation information may be stored through Orbit's cloud infrastructure and/or temporarily or locally within your browser.

You should avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the task you are asking Orbit to perform.

5. Orbit Memory

Orbit may provide a memory feature designed to remember information that can make future conversations more useful or personalized.

Memory information may include information derived from or explicitly provided during your conversations.

Depending on the current implementation, memory-related information may be stored locally in your browser and/or associated with your authenticated Orbit account through cloud storage.

Orbit provides controls that may allow you to:

  • Enable or disable memory.
  • View available saved memories.
  • Delete individual memories.
  • Clear available saved memories.

Memory functionality may change as Orbit continues to develop.

6. Files and Images

Where file or image functionality is available, you may be able to provide files or images to Orbit for processing or analysis.

The content of files and images you submit may be transmitted to Orbit's backend infrastructure and, where necessary, to third-party services used to provide the requested functionality.

Orbit's backend may process supported files in memory for the purpose of extracting or analyzing their contents.

Supported file types, file-size limits, processing methods, and available capabilities may change over time.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the legal right to provide any files or images you submit to Orbit.

Do not submit content that you are prohibited from sharing.

7. Browser Storage and Local Data

Orbit uses browser storage technologies, including local storage, to support certain application features.

Depending on the features you use, Orbit may store information in your browser such as:

  • Conversation history.
  • Active conversation identifiers.
  • Cloud-history cache information.
  • Memory information.
  • Memory preferences.
  • Application settings.
  • Theme preferences.
  • Other information necessary for the application to function correctly.

Browser-stored information generally remains on your device until it is removed by Orbit, by you through available controls, or by clearing your browser's stored data.

Clearing browser storage may cause certain Orbit settings, locally stored conversations, or other local application information to be removed.

Orbit may also rely on browser or third-party mechanisms necessary for authentication and service functionality.
